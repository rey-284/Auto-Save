Insurance, decoded

QUICK REFERENCE

Liability: the must-know

Often required by state law, liability can help pay for injuries or property damage you cause to others. A $50k / $100k / $50k limit usually means per-person injuries / injuries per crash / property damage. It usually does not repair your own car.

Collision: your car, after a crash

Collision coverage can help repair or replace your car after a covered crash, even if you caused it. You pay your deductible first.

Comprehensive: the unexpected stuff

This can cover certain non-crash losses, such as theft, hail, or a fallen branch. Check the policy for exclusions and deductible.

Deductible: your share

A higher deductible can lower a premium, but means more out of pocket for a covered repair. Pick an amount you could actually manage.

Roadside help

Towing, jump-starts, or lockout help may be available as an add-on or through another service. Compare what is included before paying twice.

Student discounts

Ask insurers about good-student, driver-training, low-mileage, or bundling discounts. Eligibility and savings vary, so compare the final price.

Borrowing a family car?

Insurance rules depend on who owns the car, where you live, and how often you drive it. Ask the policyholder and insurer before relying on coverage.

Before you hit “buy”

Compare the same liability limits, deductibles, drivers, and vehicle on every quote. Read the policy and confirm the details directly with the insurer.
